DESIGN NOTE The Fuel column is why this screen lives in FuelSphere. A price variance of 2.82 % is a number. The same variance with one click to the ticket, the delivery that measured it and the flight that burned it is a supplier conversation.

Three severities, three permissions. A warning is recorded and does not gate. A soft error gates and an authorised user may release it, with who, when and why recorded. A hard error cannot be bypassed by anyone — it needs a corrected invoice. The Bypass button is disabled on a hard row rather than hidden, so the rule is visible.
DESIGN NOTE Four tabs, not one list — two of the four are not invoices. Not billed and Claim expiring live on the delivery. A union would flatten four different things into a shape none of them has. They share a page because they share a question.
