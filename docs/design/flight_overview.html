DESIGN NOTE Nine cards, and the order is the planner's not the model's. Fuel status first because it is the question. Dispatch and aircraft next because they explain the quantity. Supplier and agent wide, because contacts are what a planner reaches for at 05:00 and time is of the essence. Orders, tickets and delivery in the sequence the fuel took. Burn wide because it carries a timeline. Invoicing last, because it arrives weeks later.

Every figure here is from the seed and recomputes. 2,305.76 is 2,884 × 0.7995. 2,305.00 is 4,202.50 − 1,897.50. The stack sums to block fuel. Block burn less trip burn is taxi, at both ends.
